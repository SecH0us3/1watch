Privacy Policy

1watch: 24h Clock & Widget

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 1watch ("we", "our", or "the app") treats user data. We believe in privacy by design, zero telemetry, and complete transparency.

1. Zero Telemetry & Offline First

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information (names, emails, phone numbers, contacts, device IDs, or IP addresses).
  • No User Accounts: The app operates without any registration, passwords, or cloud user accounts.
  • No Trackers or Advertising: 1watch contains no tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics, telemetry services, or ad networks.

2. Permissions & How They Are Used

Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

1watch requests access to your approximate location solely for two local calculations:

  • Solar Horizon Engine: To calculate astronomical sunrise, sunset, solar noon, twilight phases, and polar day/night on your 24-hour dial.
  • Hourly UV Radiation Index: To fetch accurate real-time UV index values.

All astronomical computations happen strictly on your local device. Location data is never stored on remote servers or shared with third parties.

Wallpaper & Widget Services

Required to draw the live wallpaper and update the home screen widget. The rendering engine runs locally using device hardware acceleration.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide real-time UV radiation data, the app communicates with the open-source Open-Meteo Weather API. Only approximate latitude/longitude coordinates are sent in the API request. No unique device identifiers or personal tokens are included.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All user customization preferences (theme, bezel style, font scaling, hand style) are stored locally in your device's private storage (Android SharedPreferences). Uninstalling 1watch permanently deletes all saved preferences.
