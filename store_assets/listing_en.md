## Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

**1watch** is a precision 24-hour single-hand watch, live wallpaper, and interactive home screen widget inspired by classic German 24-hour horology and the natural rhythm of the Earth.

Unlike traditional 12-hour clocks where the hour hand loops twice a day, 1watch completes **a single 360° revolution per full day**. Midday (12:00) is at the zenith, midnight (00:00) is at the nadir, and sunrise and sunset naturally align with your local horizon.
